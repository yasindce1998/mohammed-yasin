Warmor

eBPF Monitoring with WebAssembly Policies

  • Golang
  • eBPF
  • WebAssembly
  • Policy Engine

Overview

Warmor fuses two runtimes: eBPF for zero-overhead syscall monitoring in the kernel, and WebAssembly for expressing security policies as portable, sandboxed modules. Policies compile once and run anywhere — the kernel feeds events, WASM decides, and enforcement happens at the source.

What it does

> monitor.attach(syscall_trace)
> policy.load(wasm) verified
> verdict.deny(execve /tmp/x)
