Overview

A firmware research platform modeling real-world bootkit threats — BlackLotus, LogoFAIL, CosmicStrand — as reproducible lab artifacts, each paired with a working detection. Barzakh (the boundary between worlds) studies the gap between firmware and OS: UEFI implants, boot-chain persistence, and the trust boundaries that fail before the kernel ever loads.

> implant.model(blacklotus)
